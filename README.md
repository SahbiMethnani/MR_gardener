# MR Gardener&#x20;

**MR Gardener** is a responsive showcase website for a gardening and plant-selling company. The platform presents gardening services, plant collections, service packages, and company information to help customers discover available offers and get in touch with the business.

&#x20;**Live Website:** [https://mr-gardner.netlify.app/](https://mr-gardner.netlify.app/)

## Overview

MR Gardener aims to make gardening and plant selection accessible to everyone by offering carefully selected plants, personalized advice, and gardening solutions adapted to different spaces and lifestyles.

The website provides visitors with information about the company's services, available plants, pricing, and contact details.

## Features

- **Home Page:** Introduction to the company and its gardening activities.
- **Plant Catalog:** Browse available plants with descriptions and prices.
- **Gardening Offers:** Discover different gardening packages and services.
- **About Us:** Learn about the company's history, values, and approach.
- **Contact Page:** Access company contact information and submit inquiries through the contact form.
- **Responsive Design:** Interface adapted to desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
